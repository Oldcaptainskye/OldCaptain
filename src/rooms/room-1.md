A double room at the front of the house, with a black leather bed, stag-print bedding and a lamp on each side. The window looks out over the rooftops to the hills behind Portree, and the morning light comes in early. Beside the bed there is a low table with a kettle, cups, tea and coffee, and a bench for your bags.

The private shower room is through its own door inside the room: a walk-in shower, heated towel rail, hairdryer and toiletries. Flat-screen television and free Wi-Fi.

A complimentary breakfast, laid out in the kitchen from seven for you to help yourself, is included. Parking is available on request.
