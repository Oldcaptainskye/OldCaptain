A small double room away from the road and looking onto the garden. A double bed, a wall-mounted television, a bedside lamp: cosy rather than large. It is the smallest of the five, so please choose it knowing that.

**The bathroom is not inside the room.** It is private to Room 5, but it is through the next door, a couple of steps from the bedroom door, and it has a window, a hairdryer and toiletries. 

Free Wi-Fi.

A complimentary breakfast, laid out in the kitchen from seven for you to help yourself, is included. Parking is available on request.
