A small double room at the back of the house, away from the road and looking onto the garden. A double bed with red cushions, striped curtains, a wall-mounted television, a bedside lamp and a coat stand: cosy rather than large. It is the smallest of the five, so please choose it knowing that.

**The bathroom is not inside the room.** It is private to Room 5, but it is through the next door along the corridor, a couple of steps from the bedroom door. It is the only bathroom in the house with a full-size bath, and it has a window, a hairdryer and toiletries. Free Wi-Fi.

A complimentary breakfast, laid out in the kitchen from seven for you to help yourself, is included. Parking is available on request.
