# Privacy and cookies

**This page is a draft and must be reviewed before the site goes live.** The details below need confirming against how bookings are actually handled.

## Who we are

Old Captain Guest House, 1 Blaven Road, Portree, Isle of Skye IV51 9PH. You can contact us by telephone or email using the details in the footer of this site.

## What we collect

When you make a booking we collect your name, contact details, the dates of your stay and the information needed to take payment. When you contact us by email or telephone we keep what you send us so that we can reply and manage your stay.

## Payment

Card payments are handled by our booking provider and their payment processor. We do not see or store your full card details.

## Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. If a booking system is embedded on the site, that provider may set cookies necessary for the booking to work.

## How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them for accounting and tax purposes, and no longer.

## Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details in the footer. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
